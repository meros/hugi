Click and drag to draw; right-click or shift-drag to erase. The clues follow your picture, and the solver checks it after every change, in your browser. A good puzzle has exactly one solution. "No guessing needed" means row and column logic alone solves it.

Import a puzzle. Drop a file anywhere on the page, choose one, or paste text.

  • Clues: Hugi's format (rows, one clue per row, cols, one per column), a webpbn .nin export, or a .non file. The solver finds the picture.
  • A picture drawn in text: # for filled, . for empty.
  • An image (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP): it is shrunk to a grid you can adjust below.
